| `MEDULLA-BUSINESS-OPS-R002.4` | **Reconciliation matching.** Kind `reconcile`. Input: three node sets (orders, payments, payouts), an amount tolerance in minor units, a date window in days and a currency rule. Output: matches `order↔payment` and `payment↔payout` including one-to-many and many-to-one, each with its residual. Unmatched nodes list with a reason. Ties break by smaller date gap, then smaller residual, then lower node id; a tie is reported. Refunds net against their payment. Fees net against the payout. Caller chain: `Method::Uql` → `dispatch_query_family` (`crates/medulla-h-query/src/query/dispatch.rs`) → UQL `EVALUATE` stage → `crates/medulla-numeric/src/evaluation/registry.rs::evaluate` → `crates/medulla-numeric/src/business/reconcile.rs::match_sets` (new).
