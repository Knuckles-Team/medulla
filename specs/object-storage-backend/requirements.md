Caller chain: served `AddNode` → `commit_mutation_body` (`crates/medulla-server-mutation/src/mutation/commit_entry.rs:327`) → `commit_mutation_body_staged_path` (`crates/medulla-server-mutation/src/mutation/commit_paths.rs:151`) → `PersistenceBackend::commit_mutation_batch` (`crates/medulla-server-api/src/api/persistence.rs:275`; called at `commit_paths.rs:133`) → new `ObjectStoreBackend::commit_mutation_batch` in `crates/medulla-persistence/src/object_store_backend.rs`. Publication is part of the commit: it runs at `Shard::commit_batch` (`crates/medulla-store/src/redb_store/shard.rs`), the one chokepoint that compact, staged and cross-modal commits all pass through, through the `CommitPublisher` the backend attaches to every shard (`crates/medulla-store/src/redb_store/commit_publish.rs`). Every fresh commit is published as a `BatchSegmentEntry`: the caller's batch and its `CommitMaterial` (`compact`; `staged` with the authoritative state; `cross_modal` with its rows; `change_envelope` with the envelope and its source budget; `top_up` with the sealed enrichment transition; `drain` with the graph methods of a coalesced `commit_ops` group; `version_bump` for a maintenance write), so a replay re-commits by the path the original took. Every write that bumps a graph version is published: caller batches at `Shard::commit_batch`, and coalesced drains and maintenance writes at `Shard::commit_drain_with`, the same publication seam, so no version bump goes unpublished and each published entry moves the graph version by exactly one. The rows a `version_bump` write leaves (audit chain, capability and budget rows) are node-local bookkeeping and are not part of the published graph. A replay answer publishes nothing. Step 1: stage the commit in the local transaction, receipt final and not yet committed. Step 2: upload the `BatchSegment` of this commit as an immutable segment. Step 3: put `manifests/<v+1>.json` with create-only mode. Step 4: update `HEAD` with a conditional put on its current version tag. Step 5: commit the local transaction and ack the caller. Step 4 is the fence. The ack carries the existing receipt shape (`MutationBatchCommit`, `crates/medulla-protocol-types/src/mutation_batch/model/records.rs:31`). At open, the graph's local version is compared with the `graph_version` of the head manifest after the replay. A local copy ahead of `HEAD` refuses the open with `OBJECT_LOCAL_AHEAD` and is never purged. A local store behind `HEAD` replays the missing entries. Version zero of a graph with no root is bootstrapped from the local store: when the graph already holds data, its snapshot segment is the row pages of that one graph (nodes, edges and every other graph-scoped owner table, per R002); otherwise it is empty. Every manifest records the graph's `graph_name`, `graph_type` and `graph_version`, and the restore registers the graph from them (a root written without them is not read). Config: the spec's durability class decides when step 4 counts as durable; `ObjectStoreConditionalPut` means the store acknowledged the conditional put. Refusals: `OBJECT_PUBLISH_STALE` when step 4 loses the conditional put. The commit fails before the ack and the local transaction rolls back in the same transaction, so a lost fence is never acked and nothing is discarded at a later open. The node then stops publishing until it reopens from the root. `OBJECT_DURABILITY_UNDECLARED` at open for a spec without a class (the typed form of `BackendSpecRefusal::MissingDurabilityClass`). Evidence: a crash between steps 2 and 4 reopens at the prior version. The graph-copy row `MEDULLA-OBJECT-STORAGE-BACKEND-R003.2` consumes `RedbBackend::export_graph_raw_for_snapshot` (`crates/medulla-persistence/src/redb_backend/backend_shard_routes.rs:23`) and `import_graph_raw_from_snapshot` (:48). Writes: root objects only. Depends: `MEDULLA-OBJECT-STORAGE-BACKEND-R002`, `MEDULLA-DURABLE-KERNEL-R016`. | Test: `crates/medulla-persistence/src/object_store_backend/tests.rs::commit_before_ack_and_partial_publish` (planned) (L3) interrupts between steps and reopens.
