| `MEDULLA-BRANCH-MERGE-R005` | **Epistemic conflict resolution.** Class: BUILD. Subsystem: ingest, branches. Decision on the weighting: this row adds no formula. The evidence weighting is MEDULLA-DECISION-ENGINE-R828's `ResolveConflict` family, and this row only feeds it. That family scores each candidate by `ConflictCandidate::score()` (source reliability times confidence). The higher score wins when the gap is at least knob `conflict.min_margin`. A smaller gap goes to the later `valid_from` only when the windows overlap (`ConflictCandidate::overlaps`) and knob `conflict.recency_tiebreak` is 1. Otherwise the family abstains with `CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED`. `merge_conflicts` never compares scores, times or sides itself, so the rule exists once. It is `Open` with the abstention's code (`CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED`, `CONFLICT_POLICY_MISSING` or `CONFLICT_NONFINITE`) when the outcome is `Abstained`. `policy_digest` is the pinned policy's digest, or `None` for `DecisionPolicyRef::Default`.
